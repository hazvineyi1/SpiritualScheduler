## 1. What I'm building

A web application for a **single spiritual healer**, **Elliestrator Botanica** (the healer's name is "Ellie"). It is both a **booking platform** and a **personal online shop**, plus a private **healer dashboard**. Clients anywhere in the world can browse readings, book a session, pay, and upload proof of payment. Ellie verifies payments and runs her practice from a login-protected dashboard.

There are two surfaces:
1. **Client storefront** — public.
2. **Healer dashboard** — behind a login.

Provide a way to switch/preview both (a header toggle is fine for the demo; in production the dashboard is reached by logging in).

---

## 2. Users & roles

- **Client (public):** browses, books, pays, uploads proof, enters their WhatsApp number. No account required.
- **Healer (Ellie):** logs in (email + password). Sees all bookings, verifies payments, manages the calendar, sends session links, and views stats.

---

## 3. Core principle — privacy / no storage (important)

Readings and conversations are **never recorded or stored**. Surface this clearly to clients and treat it as a real data rule:
- Live sessions are not recorded.
- Session links are **single-use and expire after the session**.
- Uploaded proof-of-payment is shown to the healer **for verification only** and **deleted after** a decision is made.
- Only minimal booking records persist (who/what/when/status/amount) — no reading content, no chat transcripts.

## 5. Session formats (used throughout)

Five delivery formats. A reading supports a subset of these (see §9 rules):
- **Video Meeting** — live, scheduled. Generates a video room link.
- **Audio Call** — live, scheduled. Generates an audio room link.
- **Live Chat** — live, real-time text **delivered via WhatsApp**.
- **Asynchronous** — a private recorded/written reading delivered within the response time; **not stored** after delivery. No live link.
- **In-Person** — at Ellie's Harare studio; address shared via WhatsApp after confirmation.

Indicate the session type clearly wherever a booking appears (a colour-coded label/badge with an icon is desirable — palette is your choice).

## 6. Booking flow (client) — step by step

A multi-step flow launched from any reading:

**Step 1 — Choose format.** Show only the formats this reading supports. For 18+ readings, require an age-confirmation checkbox before continuing.

**Step 2 — Customise & schedule.**
- For **live** formats (video/audio/chat/in-person): let the client choose a **duration** (15 / 30 / 45 / 60 min) — price updates live. Then pick a **date** on a calendar and a **time slot** (times in Harare/CAT; disable past dates and Sundays).
- For **asynchronous**: let the client choose **number of questions** (1 / 3 / 5 / Unlimited) — price updates live. Show expected delivery time.
- **Fixed sessions** (see §9) are locked to 60 minutes (no duration picker) but still pick date/time.
- Pricing model: each reading's listed price = its standard (30 min / 3 questions). Shorter/fewer is cheaper, longer/more costs more, scaling from the base. Fixed sessions always charge their listed price.

**Step 3 — Intake questions.** Show the reading's intake questions (default: full name, date of birth, main question/focus; some readings have tailored questions). Answers are confidential.

**Step 4 — Pay & confirm.** This is the final step. In order:
1. **Choose payment method:** EcoCash, WorldRemit, or Remitly. On selection, show the **payment instructions** (where/how to send the amount in USD).
2. **Enter WhatsApp number** (required) — this is where the session link and confirmation will be sent.
3. **Upload proof of payment** (image or PDF receipt; show a preview).
4. **Confirm booking** — enabled only once a method is chosen **and** a number is entered **and** proof is uploaded. **Once those three are done, the single remaining action is Confirm.**

**On Confirm:**
- Create a booking with status **"pending verification"** and attach the uploaded proof, the client's WhatsApp number, the session type, the price, and the date/time (for live bookings).
- The booking immediately appears in the healer's queue / "waiting" list (§7.3).
- Show the client a clear success state: they are **in the queue**, nothing more to do, and their session link + confirmation will arrive on their WhatsApp once Ellie verifies payment.

Prices are in **USD**. A small platform/booking fee line is fine but optional.

## 7. Healer dashboard — features (login required)

### 7.1 Login
- Email + password login screen. (For the demo, accept any credentials; in production wire real auth.) Include a log-out action.

### 7.2 Stats / analytics (collect plenty)
Show a rich set of metrics, computed from real booking data where possible:
- Appointments made (all time), Completed, Upcoming, Awaiting verification.
- Payments received (total $) and number of payments, Average booking value.
- This month's earnings (+ trend vs last month).
- Cancellations, Average rating, Repeat-client rate, Average response time, Profile views.
- **Bookings by session type** (video / audio / chat / in-person / async) as a small breakdown.
- **Payments by method** (EcoCash / WorldRemit / Remitly) — count and total each.
- **Earnings trend** chart (last 6 months).
- **Most-booked readings**.
- **Completed sessions** list (record only — no reading content).

### 7.3 "Waiting now" — live display (required)
A prominent panel with a live/pulsing indicator and a **count of people currently waiting**, covering two cases:
- Clients **in the session room** waiting to be admitted to a live session → **Admit & join** action (opens the session room link).
- Clients **waiting for payment verification** → **View proof** action.
Freshly arrived bookings should be marked (e.g., "just now").

### 7.4 Payment verification
- Opening a pending booking shows the **uploaded proof of payment** (the real image/receipt the client uploaded), plus the **session type**, **date/time**, **amount**, **method**, and the client's **WhatsApp number**.
- Two actions: **Verify & send link** (approve) or **Decline**.
- On **approve**: mark the booking confirmed, then **automatically send the client a WhatsApp message** containing: payment-verified confirmation, the **session type**, the **date/time (CAT)**, and the **session link** (video/audio room link, the WhatsApp chat for chat sessions, or the studio location for in-person). For **asynchronous** readings, send a delivery confirmation instead of a link.
- After a decision, the proof image is discarded (not stored).

### 7.5 Appointments — List + robust Calendar
Provide a **toggle between List view and Calendar view**:
- **List:** all upcoming appointments, each showing client, reading, **session type (prominent)**, date/time, amount, status, and actions.
- **Calendar (make this robust):** a month calendar showing each day's appointments as compact, **colour-coded-by-session-type** entries (client + time). Highlight today. Clicking a day reveals that day's full appointment list. Include a legend. (Month navigation may be stubbed for the demo.)

### 7.6 Appointment actions (on confirmed bookings)
- **Send link:** generate an **ephemeral, single-use** session link appropriate to the format and send it to the client's WhatsApp.
- **Reschedule:** pick a new date/time (calendar + slots) and notify the client on WhatsApp.
- **Cancel:** with confirmation; notify the client on WhatsApp.

## 8. Integrations & infrastructure

- **Payments:** EcoCash (Zimbabwe domestic + USD wallet), WorldRemit, Remitly (international/diaspora). For v1, payment is **manual with proof-of-payment upload + healer verification** (as specified). Architect so a real gateway (e.g., **Paynow** or **DPO Pay**, which bridge EcoCash/cards/PayPal) and the remittance rails can be slotted in later. Price everything in **USD**.
- **WhatsApp:** For v1, use **`wa.me` deep links** (pre-filled messages) for the floating contact button, cart checkout, booking hand-off, reschedule/cancel notices, and the verified-payment confirmation + session link. Architect so the **WhatsApp Business / Cloud API** can later send these automatically.
- **Video/Audio sessions:** generate room links via an embeddable provider (e.g., Daily.co, Jitsi, Twilio). Links must be single-use and expire after the session; sessions are not recorded.
- **File upload:** accept image/PDF proof of payment; store only transiently and delete after verification.
- **Auth:** healer login.
- **Database:** persist readings, products, bookings/appointments (status, session type, date/time, amount, method, client WhatsApp number), and reviews. Do **not** persist reading content, chat, or proof images beyond verification.
- **Hosting:** web app, responsive, accessible from any country. Choose the stack you work best with on Replit.

## 13. Definition of done

1. A client can browse, pick a reading, choose a format, customise duration/questions, schedule (for live), answer intake, choose a payment method, see payment instructions, enter their WhatsApp number, upload proof, and **confirm** — and that booking appears in the healer's queue with the proof attached.
2. The healer can log in, see rich stats, see **who is waiting right now**, open a pending booking, view the uploaded proof, and **verify** — which confirms the booking and sends the client a WhatsApp message with the session type, time, and session link.
3. The healer has a **list and a robust calendar** of appointments, each clearly showing the **session type**, and can send links, reschedule, and cancel.
4. The shop works with a WhatsApp checkout.
5. Nothing about readings/conversations is stored; proof is discarded after verification; session links expire.
6. It's responsive, works from any country, prices in USD, and **looks great — your design throughout.**
